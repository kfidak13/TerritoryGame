Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Borderfall ("Borderfall", "we", "us") collects when you play the game at borderfall.fly.dev (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. Borderfall is run by the Borderfall team. If you have any question about this policy or your information, email us at fibacenterprises@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

If you play without an account (as a guest):

  • A random player ID that your browser creates and keeps in its local storage, so the game can recognise you when you reconnect.
  • The name you type in for a game.
  • Your game settings, stored only in your browser.

If you sign in with Discord or Google, we also receive and store, with your permission:

  • From Discord: your Discord user ID, username, display name, avatar, and your email address and whether Discord has verified it.
  • From Google: your Google account ID, your email address and whether Google has verified it.
  • An account ID and a public player ID we create for you, and the date your account was created.
  • Your game statistics: games played, won and lost, by game type, mode and difficulty.

We never receive your Discord or Google password.

When you play, for everyone:

  • The game's own records: the players in each game, their in-game names and actions, and the outcome.
  • Technical information our server receives with every connection, such as your IP address, browser type and the times you connect. We use it to run the game, keep it secure and fix problems, and keep it only in short-lived server logs.

2. How we use it

  • To run games and let you sign in and stay signed in.
  • To show your name and statistics on your profile.
  • To keep the game fair and safe: preventing cheating, abuse and spam, and enforcing our Terms of Service.
  • To fix bugs and improve the game.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or use your information to build advertising profiles. We use your email address only to recognise your account (for example, so signing in with Discord and with Google reaches the same account) and to answer you if you contact us.

3. Cookies and local storage

  • Sign-in cookie (bf_session): keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is set only if you sign in.
  • Sign-in check cookie (bf_oauth): protects the sign-in process and expires after 10 minutes.
  • Local storage: your guest player ID and game settings, kept in your browser.

These are all needed for the game to work. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

4. Who else is involved

  • Discord and Google, if you choose to sign in with them. Their own privacy policies apply to your accounts with them.
  • Fly.io, which hosts our servers and stores our data in the United States.
  • Cloudflare, whose Turnstile service checks that players are human before they join online games.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our players or the Service.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account information and statistics: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Server logs: a short time, typically a few weeks at most.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can play without an account at all.
  • You can sign out at any time, from this device or from all devices.
  • You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete your account and its statistics, by emailing fibacenterprises@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We will respond within 30 days.
  • You can remove Borderfall's access from your Discord or Google account settings at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom or California), you may have further rights under local law. Email us and we will help.

7. Children

Borderfall is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

8. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), store sign-in sessions only in hashed form, and limit access to the people who run Borderfall. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will let signed-in players know in the game.
